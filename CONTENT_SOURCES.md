## Valuation exam preparation

`Valuation/05 Exam Preparation/FRE6103_EXAM_01_Midterm_2025.pdf` contains the 2025A question paper on PDF pages 1–2 and key on pages 3–4. The seven practice questions map to:

| Source | Practice skill |
|---|---|
| Q1 | Shift/subtract a cash-flow series; derive with MBE/GVE |
| Q2 | Lintner cash-flow CAPM, covariance risk price, values, weights, return moments |
| Q3 | Cholesky factor and row-oriented inverse transformation |
| Q4 | Standard deviation vs standard error; quadratic sample-size scaling |
| Q5 | Critique price-level correlation and propose a better analysis |
| Q6 | Mean-reverting stochastic process with a mixture of jump sizes |
| Q7 | Capital vs cash and risk-adjusted performance |

New questions use changed inputs and original explanations. The source permits two hours, calculator and supplied formula sheet; those historical rules are not asserted for the current final.

Source-key nuances are explicitly handled:

- Q3's answer-key point allocation differs from the question paper. Practice follows the paper (15 points) for a 100-point total.
- Q6 uses inconsistent mean/intensity symbols and compressed exponential-sampling notation. Practice uses μ for mean, π for Poisson intensity, and −ln(U)/λ for the exponential draw, with separate jump arrival and size draws.
- Q7 names Sharpe/dollarized Sharpe. Practice states that course wording and distinguishes it from return-on-economic-capital/RAROC conventions rather than treating them as identical.
- Q5 does not justify a general rule that return correlation must be negative. Practice asks students to inspect returns and avoid inventing results without data.

## Supplied Derivatives paper

Two user-supplied photographs show the October 17, 2026 Introduction to Derivatives midterm: five questions, 8 points each, two hours. Originals are included as `FRE6073_Midterm_2026_Page_1.jpeg` and `Page_2.jpeg`, published with the user’s authorization.

The five worked preparation questions cover growing discrete dividends, index futures loss/spot recovery, interest-bearing margin calls, coupon-bond forward value and a six-year par swap. Solutions are independently derived; no instructor key was provided. Assumptions are explicit: year-three dividend precedes delivery, constant futures pricing rates, one-day accrual of 1/365 with interest before settlement, dirty bond price and standard single-curve swap valuation. Alternative conventions can change answers. Changed-scenario drills support the syllabus's emphasis on transfer rather than memorized copies.

The page does not infer the final's scope from this midterm. The syllabus's later options, Wiener process, BSM derivation and Greeks remain separate required study areas, with depth gaps identified.
