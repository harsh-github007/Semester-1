    How to use the books

    1. Read the everyday explanation in the app first.
    2. Open the matching lecture, then the book topic below.
    3. Try the example with the solution covered.
    4. Write the assumptions, units and one reason the method could fail.

    Reading map

    Hull, 8th edition: contracts; futures markets and hedging; interest rates; forward prices; swaps; options and trees; Wiener processes, Black–Scholes and Greeks. Match by topic: your course references a later edition.

    Shimko, Valuation Principles: MBE/GVE, cash-flow patterns, risk adjustments, simulation and stochastic processes. Chapter numbering differs across the supplied excerpts; check the section title.

    Ivo Welch, Corporate Finance: time value, capital budgeting, risk/return, CAPM and financing. The supplied book is a 4th-edition preview; its filename and internal publication date differ, so use the book’s own title and section headings.
